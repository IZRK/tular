SOS Proteus 2024 · Kranj

Past event · 21–22 May 2022 · Trieste Natural History Museum, Via dei Tominz 4, Italy

Conservation in a changing climate

The fourth meeting connected olm research, habitat conservation and the protection of karst water resources with climate challenges. It marked 260 years since Scopoli’s first scientific description in his 1762 letter to Linnaeus. The meeting language was English.
