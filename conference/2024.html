SOS Proteus 2022 · Trieste

Past event · 7–8 December 2024 · Municipality of Kranj, Slovenia

Research methods and conservation status

The fifth conference examined proteus and its karst groundwater habitat, with an emphasis on evidence for conservation assessment. It marked 30 years since the taxonomic description of black proteus and remembered Andrej Mihevc (1952–2024), who discovered it in 1986.

Research highlights

  • Monitoring olms in karst springs using infrared cameras: Meta Valenčič, Žiga Fišer, Blaž Kekec, Ester Premate and Hans Recknagel.
  • First assembled olm families from the wildlife: D. Kermek, V. Zakšek and P. Trontelj.
