# Content and link audit

Audit date: 15 September 2026.

## Original collection

The original website yielded 47 reachable URLs, including duplicate English paths and article aliases. The redesign consolidates these into 26 pages, 13 in each language, while retaining the seven main sections and subordinate articles.

The research archive contains 50 original entries per language. The source's duplicated, orphaned 2018 eDNA title at the end of the 2009 bibliography was removed; the complete 2018 record is retained. The media archive contains 117 English and 84 Slovenian records. The differences between the original language collections are preserved rather than filled with invented translations.

## Formatting corrections

- Replaced Joomla layout tables, inline fonts, colours, empty paragraphs and wrapper elements with semantic articles and responsive galleries.
- Kept headings, photographs, captions and explanations together when the original table separated them into rows.
- Decoded the publicly displayed email addresses into ordinary `mailto:` links.
- Linked bare URLs and DOI identifiers.
- Replaced repeat “read more” labels with descriptive links.
- Corrected obvious split-word and typographical errors, including `T ular`, `Šp ela`, `Aljanči č`, `decribed`, and `Environental`.
- Restored square-kilometre superscripts in homepage descriptions and removed the outdated “30 years” label from the black-proteus homepage heading.
- Separated bibliography titles, authors, citation data, source actions and expandable affiliations.
- Removed nested anchors introduced when replacing missing leaflet previews.

Historical project dates, range estimates and terminology remain identifiable as historical material. In particular, the old English and Slovenian range estimates differ. The original scientific narrative has not been silently rewritten to imply a current institutional position.

## Links

156 unique external links were checked. Original responses included 79 HTTP 200 results, 47 HTTP 404 results, and a mixture of access restrictions, server errors and unreachable hosts.

Recoveries include:

- Eleven old Natura Sloveniae PDF links, now linked to the current University of Ljubljana journal article pages.
- Moved Gorenjski glas stories, recovered at the publisher's archive.
- Five Delo articles recovered and verified by page title.
- Updated institution URLs and verified redirects, including RTV's newer archive routes.
- Permanent DOIs in place of expiring signed PDFs and obsolete early-online URLs.

**36 links remain unresolved and use an explicitly labelled “Find archived copy” action.** These actions open an Internet Archive lookup; they do not claim that a working snapshot was verified. Entries with no original online source use a clearly labelled source search. All citations remain readable without following a link.

HTTP 403/406 responses are treated as access restrictions, not proof that the article is missing. Publisher URLs with such responses remain linked when their identity is known. The complete original responses and repair decisions are in `reports/link-audit.json`.

## Editorial and layout correction

The earlier structural validation did not establish that the copy was fully proofread. A subsequent editorial pass corrected lowercase homepage titles, place names and GIS capitalization, spelling errors in both languages, malformed sentences, and stray Skype interface text. English project prose left in the Slovenian monitoring pages was translated. The original historical facts and bibliography remain, with an explicit note on an undated BirdLife link that the source archive placed under 2010.

Laboratory articles now have authored section headings and captioned figures. Species descriptions and project summaries use headings and lists. Original YouTube and Vimeo films now have responsive embedded players. The contact page has a dedicated layout and an uncropped poster; article prose and galleries use matching widths. Browser validation checks those widths and the contact image aspect ratio. It records third-party player accessibility issues separately from the site’s own markup.
