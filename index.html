Proteus

The olm, also known as the “human fish”, is an extraordinary symbol of subterranean wildlife and the long history of its study.

Conservation

Seasonal floods can wash olms out of their caves. Tular provides a sanctuary and coordinates their rescue. Call +386 31 804 163 to report a stranded olm.

Black proteus

A rare, dark-coloured olm found only in Bela krajina, southeastern Slovenia. Even local pollution can threaten its survival.

eDNA monitoring

Innovative method to survey Proteus distribution through the detection of its eDNA trace; CEPF, BirdLife International, 2013–2014; 2016

Tular Cave Laboratory

Tular is a conglomerate cave in Kranj, Slovenia. In 1960 it was turned into a cave laboratory by speleobiologist Marko Aljančič.

Projects

With Proteus we share dependence on groundwater (NGO Programme, EEA Grants & Norway Grants; 2015).
